Federal grants have been notoriously complex and difficult to understand. I led a small team working within ORR and the Office of Trafficking in Persons to develop an AI-powered chatbot using curated datasets of federal Notices of Funding Opportunity (NOFOs) and related guidance materials. We presented a working prototype to leadership, and it was one of the agency's very first AI chatbots.
