In 2020, I co-founded Open Skies Fellows to give young people in East Africa the chance to build real skills in emerging technology by leading projects that solve problems in their own communities. Working alongside OpenMap Development Tanzania, I led programming in Tanzania, Rwanda, and the Democratic Republic of Congo. The fellows have always kept the problems they are solving in mind, and the program has proven to be a powerful model for youth to gain life-changing skills.
